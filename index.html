You don't buy jobs from us. You buy weekly work on the three Google products this valley uses to find a plumber, inside a cap you set. Some weeks are quiet no matter who's running it. What you can count on is the work getting done and a page every Friday proving it.

$1,800 and no promise on jobs. What am I paying for?

The hours, photos, services, review replies, search-term cleanup, lead ratings, cap management, and Friday page that nobody at the shop has time to own. If the work ever stops showing up, you'll have the pages to prove it, and you can stop paying after the start term.

My last guy promised page one.

My guy says he disputes my bad leads.

The dispute button has been gone since 2024. Google credits leads on its own now, and it stopped crediting wrong-service and out-of-area calls entirely. The work today is rating every lead fast and setting the profile so junk never gets charged in the first place. Ask him where the button is.
